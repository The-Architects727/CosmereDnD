makes the layout easy to follow and throws away the one thing the original
filenames carried: who made the picture. That information is recorded here
instead. It must not be lost, and it must travel with the book.

## Before this book goes anywhere public

**These are other people's pictures.** Five have no artist recorded because the
filename did not carry one, and none of them has a recorded licence or
permission. That is fine for a document played at your own table and is not
fine for anything published, sold, or posted. Either clear each one with its
artist, replace it, or drop it.
